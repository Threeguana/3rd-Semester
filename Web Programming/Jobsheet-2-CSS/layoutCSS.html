INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY

In 2010, based on the needs of society and industry related to the Informatics Diploma IV Program, Polinema established a new study program: Diploma IV (D-IV) in Information Technology (IT). At the beginning, the program had only 46 students, but by 2015 the number of D-IV IT students increased to 545.

The rapid growth of students in both the D-III MI and D-IV IT programs encouraged Polinema’s leadership to merge the two programs into a new separate department independent from the Electrical Engineering Department. In 2015, based on Director’s Decree No. 53, and to improve management quality and optimize resources, the Information Technology Department (JTI) was officially established with two study programs: D-III MI and D-IV IT.
